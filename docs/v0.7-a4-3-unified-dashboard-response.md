## 1. Overview

This document specifies the internal, read-only orchestration service (`dashboardReportingService`) combining:
1. Operational data (tasks, daily reports, work modes, notifications, announcements, operational warnings).
2. Metric data (raw metric aggregation, breakdowns, daily time series, metric warnings).
3. KPI data (live KPI results, official reviews/snapshots, summaries, breakdowns, period series, KPI warnings).
4. Single filter validation and scope resolution pipeline.
5. Warning normalization across domains (`operations`, `metrics`, `kpis`, `attention`, `system`).

## 3. Cancelled KPI Preflight & Rules

- Cancelled KPI assignments may appear in status breakdowns (`by_status`).
- Cancelled KPI assignments do not contribute to achievement rate, weighted performance score, achieved count, not-achieved count, active KPI count, or missing-actual warnings.
- Cancelled records are excluded from live performance evaluation.

---

## 4. Single Filter & Scope Pipeline

- Raw filters are validated and normalized once (`validateAndNormalizeFilters`).
- Reporting scope is resolved once (`resolveReportingScope`), respecting user role (`admin`, `executive`, `manager`, `staff`, `viewer`) and preventing scope escalation.
- The same normalized filters and resolved scope are passed concurrently to operational, metric, and KPI aggregation services.

---

## 5. Warning Normalization & Partial Failure Policy

- Warnings are categorized into domains (`operations`, `metrics`, `kpis`, `attention`, `system`).
- Stable warning codes and message keys are preserved. Duplicate warnings are filtered out.
- Authorization or invalid filter failures throw 400/401/403 errors immediately.
- Optional reporting section failures trigger a partial response (`meta.partial = true`) accompanied by a system-level `partial_data_unavailable` warning.
